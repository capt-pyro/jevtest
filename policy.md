# Community Content Policy — Marketplace & Discussion Forums

_Effective for all posts, listings, comments, and direct messages on the platform._

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines what content is and is not allowed on the platform, and how
enforcement decisions are made. It is meant to be applied consistently to text
content submitted by users, taking into account both the content itself and the
posting user's history on the platform.

## 2. Violation Categories

Every piece of content is assessed against the following categories. A single
item may only be assigned **one** primary category — the one that best captures
the most serious concern present.

- **Harassment & Bullying** — content that targets a specific person or group
  with insults, threats, degrading language, unwanted persistent contact, or
  attempts to intimidate, shame, or provoke fear in them.
- **Hate Speech** — content that attacks, demeans, or dehumanizes people on the
  basis of protected characteristics such as race, ethnicity, religion, gender,
  sexual orientation, disability, or national origin. This includes slurs,
  stereotypes framed as fact, and calls for exclusion or violence against a
  group.
- **Spam & Scams** — unsolicited repetitive advertising, irrelevant links,
  engagement bait, or deceptive content designed to defraud users (fake
  giveaways, phishing links, fraudulent payment requests, impersonation of
  official accounts for financial gain).
- **Misinformation** — false or misleading factual claims presented as true,
  where the falsehood is material (e.g., health claims, safety claims, claims
  about how the platform itself works) rather than a matter of opinion or
  satire clearly framed as such.
- **Self-Harm & Dangerous Content** — content that encourages, instructs, or
  glorifies self-harm, suicide, or dangerous activities likely to cause serious
  injury to the poster or others. This does not include someone seeking help
  or support for themselves.
- **Adult / NSFW Content** — sexually explicit text or solicitation posted
  outside of any designated adult section of the platform.
- **Intellectual Property Violation** — content that reproduces copyrighted
  material without permission or that impersonates a brand/seller to sell
  counterfeit goods.
- **No Violation** — content that does not meaningfully fall into any of the
  above categories, including ordinary disagreement, criticism, negative
  reviews, profanity not directed at a person, and dark humor without a
  genuine target.

## 3. Severity Levels

Once a category is identified, severity is assessed on the following scale.
Severity should reflect the potential for harm, not merely the category name —
a low-severity example of Hate Speech is possible (e.g., a borderline
stereotype joke with no clear malicious intent), just as a high-severity
example of Spam is possible (e.g., a large-scale phishing attempt).

1. **None** — No credible violation; content is acceptable as-is.
2. **Low** — A minor or borderline issue: content that brushes up against a
   category but is ambiguous, low-reach, or clearly not malicious in intent.
3. **Medium** — A clear, unambiguous violation of one category, contained to a
   single incident, without direct threats or large-scale harm.
4. **High** — A clear violation involving explicit targeting of a person or
   group, direct threats, coordinated/large-scale scam or spam activity, or
   dangerous misinformation with real-world safety implications.
5. **Critical** — Content involving credible threats of violence, explicit
   incitement, detailed self-harm instructions, sexual content involving
   minors, or large-scale fraud actively victimizing users. Requires immediate
   action regardless of the poster's history.

## 4. Enforcement Actions

Enforcement is driven primarily by severity, but the poster's history is
always considered: someone with a record of prior warnings or suspensions
should generally receive a *more* severe action than a first-time poster with
the same content, and Critical-severity content warrants strong action
regardless of history.

- **No Action** — used when severity is None.
- **Warning** — a private notice to the user; typically appropriate for Low
  severity on a first offense, or in some cases Medium severity with a clean
  history.
- **Content Removal** — the content is taken down but the account is otherwise
  unaffected; typically appropriate for Medium severity, or Low severity from
  a repeat offender.
- **Temporary Suspension** — the account is locked for a period of time;
  typically appropriate for High severity, or Medium severity from a user with
  multiple prior warnings.
- **Permanent Ban** — the account is permanently removed; typically
  appropriate for Critical severity, or High severity from a repeat offender
  who has already received a prior suspension.

## 5. Notes on Applying This Policy

- Context matters: quoting hateful content to condemn it, or discussing a
  scam to warn others about it, is not itself a violation.
- Intent is inferred from the content and any available context, not assumed
  from category membership alone.
- When multiple categories plausibly apply, choose the one representing the
  most serious concern, and let severity capture the overall risk.
